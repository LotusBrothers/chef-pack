Chef Pack
Austin, TX

Can't decide on dinner? Rip a pack.

Every couple, group chat and roommate pair has the same fight: "I don't care, you pick." Chef Pack settles it. Pick a vibe and pay, and the app picks the restaurant and hands you a gift card for it, always worth at least what you paid. Sometimes it's upgraded.

What changed from the first idea

Pack-rip casino

  • Most packs worth less than you paid
  • Sell-back loop to keep ripping
  • Gambling law in most states
  • Payment processors and app stores push back

Dinner decider

  • Every pack worth at least the price
  • You eat the card tonight
  • The rip is the fun part, the meal is the product
  • Restaurants pay to be in packs

The MVP: four screens

  • TonightThree packs (Quick Bite, Sit-Down, Treat Yourself) plus a free "just pick for me" spin that brings people in.
  • PackThe guarantee, the upgrade odds, and one button. Apple Pay or a card.
  • RevealThe rip, then where you're eating, how far it is, and the card value. Directions in one tap, plus one swap allowed if you're not feeling it.
  • WalletYour cards with a barcode to show at the register.

Left out of v1: sell-back, pack battles, live pull feeds, leaderboards and local independents. Each one waits until people are buying packs again.
