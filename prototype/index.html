How Chef Pack makes money

  • House edgeEach pack pays out 86–90% of its price on average. The rest is margin.
  • Card discountGift cards bought in bulk from a distributor typically cost 3–15% under face value.
  • Sell-backA sell-back pays 85% as credits, not cash. Most credits get spent on the next rip, and the card never has to be bought.
  • Pity timerTen rips without a Rare guarantees one. Bad streaks end before people quit.

How we'd build it

  • AppReact Native (iOS + Android) with a matching web app. The rip and reveal animation is the core screen.
  • Odds engineServer-side, provably fair: we publish a hash of the seed before the rip and reveal the seed after it, so anyone can check the result.
  • Gift cardsA rewards API such as Tango Card or Tremendous. We buy the card the moment someone taps Keep, so there's no inventory.
  • ComplianceAge check, location blocking for restricted states, purchase limits, and a payment processor that supports sweepstakes.
  • GrowthLive pull feed, shareable grail clips, limited drops (a Valentine's date-night pack, a Super Bowl wings pack), and pack battles.
Before real money changes hands: paying for a random prize can count as gambling depending on the state. Pack-rip apps rely on a guaranteed-value prize (our floor card), a free way to enter, and location blocking. Have a sweepstakes attorney review this first.
